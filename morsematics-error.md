This is not a bug in _Morsematics_. Before performing **all** calculations and checks, you must convert any numbers outside the range of 1-26 back inside the range by adding or subtracting 26. For example, if your character pair was J and Q, J (10) + Q (17) equals 27, but since 27 is outside of the range, it must be put back inside. Subtracting 26 from 27 yields 27-26 = 1, which would count as a square number.
