There is already a very good write-up on [Playing Modded KTANE](<https://docs.google.com/document/d/1K2la3yW_l6WHXSIaK-b6WKJAsgL9TuGjx8ZhAZeHQko>) available from the repository that you should check out. This article will NOT replace it, but will act as a summary with more recent information

You will need to solve "Mission 1.3 - The First Bomb" before mods are accessible.

[This Collection of Essential Mods](<https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=925081990>) contains most of the non-module mods that everybody is expected to have. You can also download the [Colorblind Helper mod](<https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2036735094>) which might help you with all colour-based modules.

All usable Manuals for modules that are published by the community (and Vanilla modules too) is available on the Repository at https://ktane.timwi.de

There are a few ways players get used to playing with Mods in KTANE, with the most popular options being:
- [Operation Greenhorn](<https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3575597322>), a mission pack focused on keeping a feeling very similar to vanilla KTANE, with 14 modded modules introduced over 40 missions; for people who want to take their time and have a smoother experience. It also includes more exotic missions similar to the final challenges of the regular game.
- [Krow's Intro Missions](<https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2211708995>), which requires all modules in [this collection](<https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2211683331>). It introduces 155 well-known modules from the early days of modding over 90 missions, and introduces many concepts that you'll encounter often in modded KTANE: Bosses, module look-alikes, pseudo-needies, and more. [This Google Sheet](<https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/2/d/1apdLh56SEUHesn_-jTlYQBIueDuyAxfu8E9GTTMCCFk>) has a breakdown of every mission in its pack, bomb-by-bomb.
- Playing through Twitch Plays (article link TBA), though the pool of mods has been diluted over time and isn't designed for new players specifically.
