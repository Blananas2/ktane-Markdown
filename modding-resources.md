### Sources

### Libraries and Extensions

- [Link to Ktane Module Utils](https://github.com/Qkrisi/ktane-module-utils), aiming to automate processes such as new stages for Boss Modules, Coroutine Queues and more.

- [Link to wawa](https://github.com/Emik03/wawa/tree/main), a collection of libraries used to help making mods in KTANE with things such as reflection, reading/loading files, and Editor utilities.

- [redpenguiin's Polygon Generator tool](https://discord.com/channels/160061833166716928/201105291830493193/1003409611166072902)

### External Tools

- [An online linter](https://juli3.net/ktane-lint) to verify the integrity of your manuals before uploading them.

- [A fork of SVGOMG](https://luminoscity.github.io/svgomg/) a tool used to "minify" (reduce the size of/compress) SVG files

- [An online tester for Regular Expressions](https://regex101.com/)

- [An online Markdown Live Preview](https://markdownlivepreview.com/)

- [The Text Field Calculator](https://samfundev.github.io/TextFieldCalculator/Calculator-v2.html), widely used by the community

- [A reference sheet for understanding Easing Functions](https://easings.net/)

- Several websites containing free sounds, such as [Freesound](https://freesound.org/). Pay attention to the Licenses!!!
